

Whenever you need a person to decide something, they'll read a brief, not your report, so it has to stand alone. They haven't seen the code or this conversation. Put it first in your report under a `## Decision` heading, and use the same lines in `{run}/ask.md` if you ask a question. Five lines, one each, under 15 words, in plain words, with no code or file paths unless the decision turns on them:

What: what this is about
Why: why it matters
Background: the one fact they need
Bottom line: what you recommend
Ask: the question, one they can answer in a word or a sentence

If there's more than one thing to decide, don't fold them into one ask. Start with one line, `Overview:`, saying how they fit together, then write those five lines for each decision, most important first.
